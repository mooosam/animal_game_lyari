# Video reference pass — version 0.4.0

The supplied `C:/Users/Home/Downloads/dino_dash_game.mp4` is 100.8 seconds long. Key frames were reviewed across both attempts at level 112, including ordinary running, descending gaps, falling stones, transformation choices, restart, and finish. The YouTube Short at https://www.youtube.com/shorts/r0qT32CySDs was also inspected directly in the browser, including the flying, pillar, and cliff sections in level 118.

The implementation now follows the observed composition: a diagonal rear camera at a fixed player-relative distance, a large foreground animal, a floating YOU label and pointer, three compact beveled stone buttons with large animal portraits and a lime selection rim, a small level heading, and a single progress line containing every racer's position. The persistent bottom panel and obstacle signposts were removed. Foreground animals keep their colors while distant hills and jungle fade into mint haze.

Courses use sandy paths with subtle running grooves and irregular grass edges, broad cliff faces with jagged caps and grassy ledges, lavender boulder walls, turquoise animated water, falling-stone fields, and tree traversals. Opponents have their own names and tints. Original animal models, original course geometry, and original UI artwork are used; the reference's dinosaur artwork, branding, ads, and purchases are not included.

Ordinary running speed depends on the chosen animal. Correct abilities traverse obstacles quickly. Players can transform during a crossing; progress is retained rather than restarted, and changes in race speed cannot rewind the traversal. Gentle mode retains safe stops and hints for incorrect choices, including choices made in midair. Challenge mode retains its two-second setback and first-place progression rule. Ideal runs remain within the agreed 45–75 second range, approximately 48–63 seconds across the ten courses.

This is a close adaptation of the observable presentation and mechanics, not a claim of pixel-identical scenery or identical animation: the characters are animals and the ten courses are original. See `tools/screenshots/` for phone/tablet views and the recorded gameplay preview, and `VALIDATION.md` for verification and hardware limits.

The follow-up animal pass uses the user's `EverythingLibrary_Animals_002.blend` throughout. Its Rhino, Snowy Owl, Green Frog, Otter, Elephant, Tortoise, and Chimpanzee replace the previous models. The source pack has painted meshes but no skeletons or animations, so game exports include new articulated rigs and species-specific locomotion/ability clips. Natural colors are retained in baked albedo textures; the user is racer zero, with a fixed camera target and unchanged camera angle. Denser woodland, fern clusters, flowers, bark, rolling terrain, and bank rocks fill out the scene while retaining the reference's golden road and mint atmosphere. The pasted animal/obstacle list was treated as design examples, rather than an instruction to expand the roster to fifty animals.
